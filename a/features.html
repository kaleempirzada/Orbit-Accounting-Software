Zero-Latency Local Database

Unlike web-based accounting tools that buffer with every click, Orbit utilizes a high-performance local SQLite database. This means generating a year-end ledger, pulling up an invoice history, or searching through thousands of inventory items happens in milliseconds.

  • No Internet Required: Continue billing even during network outages.
  • Instant Search: Find any transaction dynamically as you type.
  • Automated Backups: Secure your local files easily without relying on external servers.
