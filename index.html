Core Features

Everything Your Business Needs

From invoicing to inventory, Orbit covers the complete billing workflow for Indian SMEs.

🧾

GST Invoicing

Create Sale, Purchase, Credit Note, Debit Note and Challan invoices with auto GST calculation — inclusive or exclusive.

📦

Inventory Management

Real-time stock tracking per item and batch. Prevents negative stock on sales with live warnings.

🏷️

Barcode Scanner

Plug-and-play barcode scanner support for ultra-fast billing at the counter. Press F4 to activate instantly.

📋

Challan Management

Save invoices as Sale or Purchase Challans. Convert them to final invoices with one keystroke (F9).

💳

Payment Tracking

Record Cash, Credit and Online payments. Maintain party ledgers with full outstanding balance tracking.

💸

Expense Management

Log business expenses by category and date. View expense summaries in monthly and annual reports.

🔐

Google 2FA Login

Admin PIN + Google Authenticator TOTP 2FA for maximum security. Reset PIN securely with OTP verification.

👥

Multi-Role Access

Separate Admin and Operator roles. Operator access is view-only to protect sensitive data and prevent edits.

🖨️

PDF & Print

Generate professional A4 and thermal invoice PDFs. Multiple print templates including Tally-style formats.
