Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Orbit Accounting Software, provided by Universal Digital Store ("we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our software.

2. Offline First & Local Storage

Orbit Accounting Software is designed as an offline-first application. This means:

  • All your business data, including invoices, inventory, parties, and financial records, are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not upload, sync, or backup your business data to our servers.
  • You have full ownership and control over your data. Ensure you maintain regular local backups.

3. Information We Collect

While your business data stays local, we may collect minimal information required for licensing and software improvement:

  • License Verification: Information related to your Microsoft Store purchase to validate your subscription status (Quarterly, Yearly, or Lifetime).
  • Crash Reports: Anonymous diagnostic data and error logs to help us fix bugs and improve performance (only if you opt-in via Windows settings).

4. Google Authenticator Integration

Our software uses Google Authenticator for 2FA (Two-Factor Authentication). The secret keys generated for TOTP are stored locally in your encrypted database. We do not transmit these keys to any external servers.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
